      Pricing, travel and follow-up

      Rules and edge cases
      • The list shows requests from the time range you pick above it (2 weeks, 1 month or 2 months). Sort by value (the package subtotal, biggest first) or by when they reached out.
      • Chips: Not replied yet, Reply needed (they wrote last), Follow-up 1, 2 or 3 (due after 3, 7 and 14 days of quiet), Date passed, Invoice paid, On calendar, and Conflict.
      • Conflict means another job is on your calendar that day. It shows in the list and above the email. Nothing is declined for you.
      • Email opens the right email: the first reply with the quote, a reply to their last message, or the next follow-up. Not available and No thanks are one click away.
      • Archive moves a lead to the bottom. Nothing is deleted.
      • County and drive time come from the address. A short or incomplete address is matched to the most likely full one. Travel fee comes from the tiers in settings, and over 90 minutes needs a manual number.
      • Event type: wedding, rehearsal dinner, corporate, birthday, anniversary, graduation or fundraiser when the form says so, otherwise “event”.
      • Subject: Name’s event on Weekday, Month D, YYYY (N guests, Tent or Truck). The package is left out for Help me choose.
      • Pricing: base covers the first 45 guests, then the per-guest rate. Caesar salad, cannoli and caprese skewers are per guest and added when mentioned. Any gluten mention adds the gluten-free line.
      • A paid invoice with no matching calendar event puts a red number on Event Planner and a banner there.
